# Coffee-Shop-Sales-Analysis
Analyzed coffee shop sales data using Power BI to understand revenue, orders, product performance, store performance and sales trends.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data to understand business performance, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and sales trends.

The main objective is to identify popular products, high-performing store locations, peak sales periods, and overall business performance using Microsoft Power BI.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze total sales and order performance.
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Understand store-wise sales performance.
- Identify peak sales hours and busy periods.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Dataset inspection and preparation.
- **Power BI:** Data transformation, analysis, and dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and business KPIs.

## Dataset Information

- Original Dataset: `Coffee Shop Sales(2).xlsx`
- Data Domain: Coffee Shop Sales
- Key Information: Transactions, product details, quantities, prices, dates, times, and store locations.

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following activities were performed:

1. Imported the Excel dataset into Power BI.
2. Checked column names and data types.
3. Reviewed missing and inconsistent values.
4. Prepared date and time fields for analysis.
5. Created calculated measures using DAX.
6. Organized the data for dashboard development.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Overall revenue generated |
| Total Orders | Total unique transactions |
| Total Quantity | Total products sold |
| Average Order Value | Sales divided by total orders |
| Average Selling Price | Sales divided by total quantity |

## Power BI Dashboard

The project contains three interactive dashboard pages.

### 1. Overall Performance

### 2. Product Performance Analysis

### 3. Store & Time Analysis

## Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Total sales were approximately **$699K**.
- Total orders were approximately **149K**.
- Total quantity sold was approximately **214K**.
- Average Order Value was approximately **$4.69**.
- Average Selling Price was approximately **$3.26**.
- June recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **$166K**.
- Coffee was the highest-revenue product category, generating approximately **$270K**.
- Sales activity was highest around **10 AM**.

*Figures are approximate dashboard values.*

## Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products.
- Focus on popular product categories to improve sales.
- Plan staff availability around peak business hours.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to improve inventory planning.
- Compare store performance to identify improvement opportunities.
